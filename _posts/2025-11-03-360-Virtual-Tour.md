<h3><strong>Project Overview</strong></h3>
Created an interactive virtual tour that familiarizes students with the kitchen and its equipment before their first day of class. The project was supported by a $2,000 Open Education Incentive (OPEN) Program grant awarded to the team to develop openly licensed learning resources.
