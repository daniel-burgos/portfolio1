<h3><strong>Overview</strong></h3>
Created an interactive virtual tour that familiarizes students with the kitchen and its equipment before their first day of class. The project was supported by a $2,000 Open Education Incentive (OPEN) Program grant awarded to the team to develop openly licensed learning resources.

<h3><strong>Challenge</strong></h3>
Students with little or no culinary experience often spent valuable class time locating equipment, navigating the kitchen and learning how to use unfamiliar tools. Giving students a chance to explore the space in advance could help them arrive better prepared and let the professor spend more class time on hands-on instruction.

<h3><strong>Approach</strong></h3> 
Working with the culinary professor and her graduate student, I helped develop a 360 virtual tour of the kitchen lab. I photographed the space using a GoPro to create the 360 experience and produced instructional videos demonstrating how to use key equipment. Using Articulate Storyline, I then embedded the videos at relevant workstations throughout the tour, allowing students to explore the space and follow a guided walkthrough of key procedures and practices while using the equipment. 

<h3><strong>Outcome</strong></h3>
The completed tour provided students with an openly accessible learning resource they could explore before class and revisit as needed. The grant-funded project also demonstrated how interactive virtual experiences could support additional online learning opportunities across the College.
