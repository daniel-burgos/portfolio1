<h3><strong>Overview</strong></h3>
Directed and produced a series of student testimonial videos highlighting why current students chose to attend the Hilton College. The campaign was designed to support recruitment efforts by showcasing authentic student experiences and personal motivations for selecting UH over other options.
<h3><strong>Challenge</strong></h3>
Prospective students often receive large amounts of information about academic programs but have limited opportunities to hear directly from current students. The college needed authentic stories that would help prospective students connect personally with the program and understand the value of the Hilton College experience.
<h3><strong>Approach</strong></h3>
I conducted interviews with current students and directed the production of a series of testimonial videos used during recruitment events and across social media channels. The featured video includes three students, while the larger campaign involved approximately six students across five separate videos. Through a combination of structured interviews and documentary-style storytelling, the videos focused on the central question: "Why did you choose the Hilton College?"
<h3><strong>Outcome</strong></h3>
The completed videos provided the college with a collection of authentic student stories that could be leveraged across multiple recruitment initiatives. By emphasizing personal experiences the campaign helped prospective students understand what it was like to be in the program.
