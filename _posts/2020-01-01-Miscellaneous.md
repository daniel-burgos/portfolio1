<h3><strong>Overview</strong></h3>
Created a collection of cinematic video intros and multimedia enhancements designed to elevate the visual quality of online course content and strengthen the Hilton College brand experience.
<h3><strong>Challenge</strong></h3>
Online lectures can sometimes feel repetitive or impersonal, particularly when students consume large amounts of video content throughout a semester. Finding ways to capture attention and create a professional first impression can help increase engagement and reinforce institutional identity.
<h3><strong>Approach</strong></h3>
As my interest in video production grew, I began developing custom lecture intros using Final Cut Pro, campus B-roll footage, and royalty-free music. Each intro is tailored to the length and purpose of the lecture, allowing instructors to set the tone before instruction begins. While not intended to teach specific content, these segments function as creative branding elements that add energy and professionalism to course materials.
<h3><strong>Outcome</strong></h3>
The resulting intros provide a polished opening experience that enhances the overall presentation quality of online courses. By incorporating visual storytelling and professional production techniques, the videos contribute to a stronger institutional brand while creating a more engaging experience for learners from the moment a lecture begins.
