<h3><strong>Overview</strong></h3>
Redux Teaching is a personal passion project created to arm educators with an instructional design process. The channel is intended to guide teachers and trainers through the curriculum development process using the Dick and Carey instructional design model, helping them create more effective and purposeful learning experiences.
<h3><strong>Challenge</strong></h3>
Teachers face significant time constraints and often receive limited formal training in instructional design. From the outside it seems like all teachers do is teach, but much of an educator's work occurs behind the scenes through planning, analysis, curriculum development, and continuous improvement. Many educators could benefit from a systematic design process. It is front workload heavy, but once the initial investment is made, subsequent years require less and less development time. Its utility increases with each subsequent year of usage.
<h3><strong>Approach</strong></h3>
Drawing from my experience as both a classroom teacher and instructional designer, I created Redux Teaching as an independent initiative focused on making instructional design concepts accessible to educators. The channel's planned series follows the Dick and Carey model from identifying instructional goals through iterative evaluation and revision. The content is specifically designed for K-12 educators, helping translate instructional design theory into practical strategies that can be applied in real-world classroom environments.
<h3><strong>Outcome</strong></h3>
Although the project is still in its early stages, Redux Teaching represents a long-term commitment to supporting educators and sharing best practices in curriculum development. The initiative reflects my passion for professional development and my belief that instructional design principles can significantly improve teaching effectiveness. The project also demonstrates my ability to independently develop educational content from concept through production.
