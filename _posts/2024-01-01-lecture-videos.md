<h3><strong>Overview</strong></h3>
Produced a diverse collection of educational lecture videos designed to extend learning beyond the classroom and improve student access to expert knowledge. Formats include direct instruction lectures, podcast-style discussions, industry expert interviews, and professionally produced virtual guest presentations.
<h3><strong>Challenge</strong></h3>
Guest speakers and industry professionals often cannot attend courses at the exact time students are available. Valuable information presented during one-time lectures is frequently lost once the session concludes. The college needed a way to preserve and scale access to expert insights while maintaining high production quality and student engagement.
<h3><strong>Approach</strong></h3>
I developed a video production process that supports multiple instructional formats. Direct instruction recordings are produced using a green screen studio, professional lighting, teleprompters, shotgun microphones, and post-production editing in Final Cut Pro.<br>
For conversation-based recordings, I coordinate podcast-style productions using multiple cameras, condenser microphones, and professional audio recording equipment. For virtual guests, I conduct pre-production meetings to optimize recording quality, provide submission workflows through OneDrive, and edit multiple video sources into a polished final product. My responsibilities include production setup, recording, editing, and final distribution through Canvas and other requested platforms.
<h3><strong>Outcome</strong></h3>
These videos have expanded access to industry expertise and instructional content by transforming one-time learning experiences into reusable educational assets. Students gain the flexibility to revisit information as needed, while instructors can leverage high-quality recordings across multiple semesters. The project demonstrates a commitment to professional production standards that enhance both the learning experience and the perceived quality of online education.
